Best Guacamole Recipe

Servings: 4 · Active: 10 min · Total: 10 min

Ingredients

  • 3 avocados (ripe)
  • ¼ cup diced red onion
  • ¼ cup finely chopped fresh cilantro
  • Zest and juice of 2 limes
  • 1 small jalapeño pepper (diced)
  • ½ teaspoon sea salt (plus more to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin (optional)
  • optional: 1 to 2 Roma tomatoes (seeded and diced)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the avocados, onion, cilantro, lime zest and juice, jalapeño, salt, and cumin, if using. Mash until the ingredients are combined but still a bit chunky.
  2. Fold in the tomatoes, if using.
  3. Season to taste and serve.
