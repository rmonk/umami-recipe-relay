Creamy Tuscan Chicken Tortellini Is the 30-Minute, One-Pot Dinner Everyone Needs Right Now

Servings: 6 · Active: 15 min · Total: 30 min

Ingredients

  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 cup julienne-cut sun-dried tomatoes packed in oil, plus 2 tablespoons sun-dried tomato oil, plus more tomatoes for garnish
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more for garnish
  • 1/4 red pepper flakes
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock
  • 2 (10-ounce) packages refrigerated cheese tortellini (about 5 cups)
  • 4 ounces fresh baby spinach (about 4 cups)
  • 3 cups shredded, cooked chicken (from a rotisserie chicken or leftovers)
  • 1/2 cup (1 ounce) freshly grated parmesan cheese, plus more for garnish
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, cubed

Instructions

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and the sun-dried tomato oil and cook, stirring constantly, until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes, oregano, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes are tender, 2 minutes.
  2. Add the cream and stock to the pan and bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat. Stir in the tortellini, spinach, and chicken and cover with a tight-fitting lid. Cook, covered and stirring occasionally, until the tortellini is cooked, the chicken is warmed through, and the spinach is wilted, 5 to 6 minutes.
  3. Reduce the heat to low. Add the parmesan and butter and toss until the butter and cheese are melted and the sauce is glossy, about 1 minute. Divide evenly among 4 serving bowls and garnish with additional sun-dried tomatoes, black pepper, and parmesan cheese, if desired. Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. Reheat gently over low heat, adding a splash of chicken broth, milk, or cream to loosen the sauce if needed.
